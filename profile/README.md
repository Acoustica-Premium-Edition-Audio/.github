## What Is Acoustica Premium Edition

Acoustica Premium Edition is Acon Digital’s Windows audio workstation. It is broader than ClickRepair and broader than a vinyl recorder. Acoustica Premium Edition audio editor is the timeline. Acoustica Premium Edition restoration suite is the repair rack. Acoustica Premium Edition spectral editing is the microscope. People open it for podcasts they host, field recordings they made, vinyl transfers they already captured, and stems they bounced from another DAW.

The Premium seat is the one with the restoration family included. That matters if you are tired of sending a WAV to a one-trick declicker, then to a denoise, then back. Spectral editing is the reason some editors stay here: a problem that is a stain on the spectrogram can be reduced without a blunt gate.

Acoustica Premium Edition is not a streaming catalog and not a sample store. Work on recordings you captured, clients files you are hired to clean, or archives you administer. Keep unprocessed versions. The workstation will not invent a performance you never recorded.

## Starting with Acoustica Premium Edition

Prerequisites: A PC you administer, audio files you already recorded or were given to restore, and optional interface I/O.

1. **Prepare:** Copy the source WAV to a working folder so the archive file stays untouched.
2. **Configure:** Open Acoustica Premium Edition audio editor, set sample rate to match the file, and disable processors you will not use on this job.
3. **Test:** Loop a noisy phrase through Acoustica Premium Edition restoration suite and compare bypass.
4. **Refine:** If the problem is a single event, switch to Acoustica Premium Edition spectral editing instead of a full-band denoise.
5. **Apply:** Render a new master from Acoustica Premium Edition workstation, named as a version, not as a replace.
6. **Maintain:** Keep notes on processor settings that worked for this mic or this transfer chain.
